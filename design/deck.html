Design notes

Hero — Elevate your Movement

Fullscreen video background (KC-Hero5.mp4) with a bg-black/40 overlay. Three-layer iOS & Android autoplay fix: setAttribute("muted",""), immediate play(), canplay retry, and touchstart fallback.

Animated counter uses requestAnimationFrame with a cubic ease-out curve to build from 0 → 5,000+ on load. Eyebrow, headline, paragraph, CTA buttons and stat all centre on mobile via Tailwind responsive classes.
